This study aimed to identify the subtypes of life satisfaction among older adults during the early and later stages of COVID-19 and examine each subtype’s transition pattern. The results of latent profile analysis revealed three latent profiles of the life satisfaction(low, moderate, high) in both the early and later stages of COVID-19, and the characteristics of these profiles remained consistent over time. Household net financial assets, activities of daily living, depression and participation in social gatherings had a significant effect on classification at both stages. Latent transition analysis results indicated that participation in social gatherings was the sole significant factor influencing the transitions between latent profiles. This study examined the changes in life satisfaction among the elderly during COVID-19 using longitudinal data. Furthermore, by identifying the crucial impact of social activity on life satisfaction, the study highlighted the importance of proactive support to encourage social participation among the elderly.
